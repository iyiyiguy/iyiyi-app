iYiYi: a digital ID for real life

An app that shows everyone's social media within 150 feet. Open it at an event, a meetup or anywhere, and see who's around you with their Instagram, TikTok and YouTube one tap away. Local businesses use it so people nearby can find and follow them. Live on iPhone and Android.

How it works

  1. Open the app at an event, a meetup or anywhere.
  2. See who's around you, from 150 feet out to citywide and nationwide.
  3. Tap anyone to jump to their Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more.
  4. Businesses get found: people nearby can discover and follow local shops and brands.

02

A creator payment network

Tip any creator, on any platform, with crypto: YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Kick, X and Instagram. One wallet collects a creator's tips from every platform, and no platform takes a cut. Tips are paid in USDC, a digital dollar, over fast, low-fee networks like Base, Solana, Arbitrum and Polygon.

For fans

  1. Sign up and add money to your account. That balance is what you use to pay creators.
  2. Watch any creator on YouTube, TikTok, Twitch, Kick, X or Instagram.
  3. On your phone: tap Share on any video or post, pick our app, and choose an amount.
  4. On desktop: install the browser extension, and it pays creators a few cents per minute while you watch, up to a monthly limit you set. The automatic streaming payouts work on desktop only.

For creators

  1. Fans can tip you before you've ever signed up. The money is locked safely under your account name.
  2. You sign in with your YouTube, TikTok, Twitch or X account to prove it's you.
  3. Everything that was waiting is sent to your wallet, and new tips arrive instantly from then on.

The key idea is the link: the video URL tells us whose content it is, and signing in with that account proves the creator owns it.

Good Gas: turning trash into energy

A gasification machine turns landfill waste (tires, paper, wood, crop waste, food scraps) into syngas, which can be made into electricity, heat, fertilizer or hydrogen fuel. One machine can process 50 to 100 tons of waste a day, cutting landfill pollution and methane while producing energy to sell. Each machine costs about $1M.
