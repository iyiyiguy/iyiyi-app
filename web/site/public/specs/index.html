SPECS

    Why Snap owning iYiYi matters

    New ad dollars, from every platform.

    CROSS-PLATFORM

    Reach beyond Snapchat

    iYiYi profiles link Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and more, so Snap can leverage user data from all those platforms in addition to Snapchat.

    REAL-WORLD SIGNAL

    Where people actually meet

    Opt-in location and linked socials show where people gather and who they follow. That's real-world context advertisers can't get anywhere else.

    SMB

    Small businesses & creators

    Cafés, gyms, shops and local creators become new paying customers, which fits Snap's push toward small and medium advertisers.

    Large brands make up most of traditional ad spend. The growth is in small and medium businesses.Paraphrasing Evan Spiegel on Tiger Sisters
    ARCADE

    In-game products

    Weapons, upgrades and characters for Laser Tag and the other Lenses.

    ENGAGEMENT

    A reason to go outside

    Games played with friends in person bring people back day after day, and give Specs a daily habit.

    ANALYTICS

    Reach back out to the people who showed up

    Businesses see who walked in and creators see who came to their show, and both can follow up with them, with insights on impressions, profile taps, follows and link clicks.

    More Lenses

    Beyond games. Specs for every day.

    Six more Lenses, also seen first person: music, the world around you, fitness and food.
