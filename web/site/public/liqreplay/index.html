How to use it

Press play to watch liquidation levels build up and get swept as price moves. Scroll your mouse wheel over the chart, drag across it, use the slider, or press ← → to scrub (hold Shift to jump 10 frames). Space plays and pauses. Each frame is one 5-minute, 15-minute or 1-hour bar.

Cyan bands are prices where longs would be liquidated (they sit below price). Pink bands are where shorts would be liquidated (above price). The brighter the band, the more money is estimated there. The panel on the right shows the levels at the current frame.

What this is (and isn't)

Like every liquidation heatmap, this is an estimate. No exchange publishes where its traders' liquidation prices or stop losses are, so nobody has that data. LiqReplay estimates it the standard way: when open interest rises, new positions are assumed to open near that bar's price, split between longs and shorts using the long/short account ratio and taker buy/sell volume, spread across 5×–100× leverage. Levels are removed when price trades through them (liquidated) or when open interest falls (positions closed). The first part of each replay starts empty and fills in as positions are added.
