iYiYi 1.5.0 — make music and better videos:
• Studio: a drum machine and sampler with 16 pads, 8 kits (Trap, Boom Bap, House, Lo-fi, ATL 808, Church Keys, Piano Bells, Jersey Club), step sequencer and Auto beats
• Sample any sound: auto-chop it onto pads, fine-tune every chop, or press Jersey Club flip for an instant beat
• Export your beats as sounds, private or public, with tags; public sounds are copyright-checked
• Add a sound while you shoot: search, trending, saved sounds and preview before you add
• New video editor: trim, crop, captions in different fonts, colors and styles, and sound volume
• New post screen to add a description with #hashtags and @mentions
• Hold the shutter to record
• Live green screen: put yourself on a beach, in Vegas, in space or on your own photo
• Music: upload songs, make playlists, show them on your profile and send songs to friends
• Sound pages with plays and the videos that use each sound
