## Why this wording
- The name and subtitle carry the highest-weight search terms: "grow", "local", "socials", "followed", "people nearby".
- The first three lines of the description are what people see before "more", so they state the benefit (followers from the people around you) and the audience (businesses and creators).
- The Arcade gets its own section and the "laser" and "tag" keywords, so the app also shows up in searches for real-world games.
